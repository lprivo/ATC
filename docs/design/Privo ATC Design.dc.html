Layout decisions

What changes from the prototype, and why
01 · FULL-HEIGHT RADAR

The scope fills the screen and runs under the top bar and the sheet. The airport sits above the middle so the area the sheet covers is the least useful one. You get about 70% more radar than the square layout did.

02 · CONTROLS IN THE THUMB BAND

Every frequent control sits in the bottom third: steppers, sim speed, re-centre. The top bar only shows information, plus pause. Pause is the one thing you should have to reach for.

03 · ZOOM + SNAP FOR PARALLELS

Pinch-zoom/pan the radar. While you draw, a loupe enlarges the area under your finger. The path snaps magnetically to the nearest threshold, which lights up, and a confirm chip lets you swap L/R before the clearance is issued.

04 · LABELS THAT GET OUT OF THE WAY

There are 16 candidate label slots in two rings around each plane, scored for overlap and kept clear of the heading vector, with leader lines when a label is pushed out. Above 8 planes, unselected labels shrink to callsign + trend.

05 · SHAPE BEFORE COLOUR

Conflict is a dashed ring, an inverted label and a “!” chip. Clearance is a solid line, an arrowhead and a runway chip. Selection adds bracket marks. Colours are amber / mint / steel, so no state depends on telling red from green.

HOW IT BEHAVES
Posture switch. When the hinge angle drops below about 150°, the layout snaps to this split. Laid flat, the radar takes about 70% of the height and the lower panel collapses to a strip row plus steppers.
Flight strips. Every airborne plane as a 42 pt row: callsign, type, altitude with trend and target, speed, and a status chip. Tap a row to select it (the radar highlights it too). Conflicts sort to the top in amber. Long-press a row to centre the radar on that plane.
Radar half. It stays fully interactive (tap, hold-and-draw, pinch). The bottom sheet is gone, so the safe area is just the top bar, and labels have room to show in full more often.
Hinge. No controls within 12 pt of the crease. The top bar sits on the radar half; pause stays top-right.
Assumption: book-style foldable with an inner screen of roughly 7.8", 4:3, rotated so the hinge runs horizontally. Final sizes will follow whatever Apple's real display and posture APIs turn out to be.

REACH & GESTURES
Bottom third: steppers, sim speed, re-centre, confirm chip. Top: information only, plus pause. Gestures: tap = select · hold 250 ms + drag = route · one-finger drag on empty = pan · pinch = zoom · double-tap empty = zoom to runways ×2.5 · tap empty = deselect. Steppers stay as buttons (more precise than gestures under pressure); swipe-to-scrub is an extra for experts.
PERFORMANCE
The radar is two canvas layers: a static layer (rings, fixes, runways, centrelines) redrawn only on zoom/pan, and a dynamic layer at 30 fps. No blur, no shadows, no gradients on the radar; the label plate is a solid fill. Label placement runs at 2 Hz. Panels are DOM, animate transform only, and are fully opaque.
COMFORT ON 6 INCHES
The sim drops to ×½ while you draw and to ×1 on a conflict. The selected plane is auto-kept above the sheet. An edge arrow points to the selected plane when it's off-screen. 375×667 (SE): sheet steppers shrink to 48 pt, top bar keeps all four items, and labels default to Min above 6 planes.
